Composition scheme vs regular GST — which fits you?

Lower tax. Quarterly filings. Sounds great — until you can't claim ITC, can't sell across states, and lose B2B clients. Here's the honest math.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Composition = simplified GST for small businesses. Pay fixed % of turnover. No ITC. No inter-state. No tax invoice (only bill of supply).
  • Rates: Manufacturers / traders 1%, restaurants 5%, service providers 6%.
  • Eligibility: turnover ≤ ₹1.5 cr (₹75L for some states). Services: ≤ ₹50L.
  • Best for: small B2C local businesses (kirana, local restaurant, small retailer).

Who can opt for composition?

  • Manufacturers (most — exceptions: ice cream, pan masala, tobacco, aerated waters)
  • Traders (any goods)
  • Restaurants (not serving alcohol)
  • Service providers under Section 10(2A) — up to ₹50L turnover

Who CAN'T opt

  • Inter-state suppliers
  • E-commerce operators
  • Suppliers of non-taxable / exempt goods
  • NRI taxable persons / casual taxable persons
  • Manufacturers of ice cream, pan masala, tobacco, aerated waters

Worked example — Kirana store

Local kirana, ₹80L turnover. All B2C walk-in customers. No inter-state. No e-commerce.

Rule of thumbB2C local low-value-add business → composition. B2B / inter-state / e-commerce / high-margin → regular.

How to opt for composition

  1. New registration

    While applying for GSTIN, select "Composition" in REG-01 → Reason for registration.

  2. Existing taxpayer wanting to switch

    File Form CMP-02 on gst.gov.in before 31 March (for next FY) → Services → Registration → Application to Opt for Composition Levy.

  3. Reverse ITC on stock

    If switching from regular: file ITC-03 within 60 days, reverse ITC on input stock as on the date of switch.

  4. Update billing

    Switch from "Tax Invoice" to "Bill of Supply". Mention "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies".

Compliance under composition

  • Quarterly CMP-08 — statement-cum-challan, due 18th of next month after quarter
  • Annual GSTR-4 — due 30 April of next FY
  • Display "composition taxable person" notice on premises and bills
  • Bill of supply (not tax invoice)
  • Maintain records of stock, purchases, sales

Composition trades GST simplicity for business flexibility. Small local? Take simplicity. Growth-oriented? Take flexibility.
