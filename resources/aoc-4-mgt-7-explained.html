AOC-4 and MGT-7 — every Pvt Ltd's annual must-do

Once a year. Two forms. ₹100/day penalty if you skip. Here's everything in plain English.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • AOC-4 = your annual financial statements filed with ROC. Due within 30 days of AGM (typically by 30 Oct).
  • MGT-7 = your annual return (members, directors, shareholding). Due within 60 days of AGM (typically by 29 Nov).
  • Late fee: ₹100 per day per form, no upper cap.
  • Repeated non-compliance can lead to director disqualification and company strike-off.

What is AOC-4?

It's the form through which you submit your audited financial statements (balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, notes) to the Registrar of Companies (ROC). The ROC stores this in the public domain — anyone can look up a company's financials on MCA21.

If your turnover or paid-up capital is small, you file AOC-4. Larger companies use AOC-4 XBRL (structured data format). OPCs file AOC-4 too.

Attachments with AOC-4

  • Board's Report
  • Audited financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Notes)
  • Auditor's Report
  • Statement of subsidiaries (Form AOC-1) — if any
  • Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) report — if applicable

What is MGT-7?

It's the annual return of the company — a snapshot of who owns it, who runs it, what changed during the year.

  • Registered office, principal business activities
  • Members (shareholders) and their shareholdings as on AGM date
  • Directors and KMP (Key Managerial Personnel)
  • Indebtedness — secured / unsecured loans
  • Penalties / compounding offences (if any)
  • Meetings held during the year (Board, AGM, EGM)

Small companies and OPCs file the simpler MGT-7A instead.

⚠️
Late filing penalty₹100 per day per form. No upper cap. A 1-year delay on both forms = ₹100 × 365 × 2 = ₹73,000. Continued non-filing for 2+ years risks director disqualification and company strike-off.

Step-by-step — the AOC-4 process

  1. Finalise books and get them audited

    Year-end is 31 March. Books should be ready and auditor sign-off complete by August/September.

  2. Board meeting to approve financials

    Board approves the financial statements + Board's Report. Resolution recorded in minutes.

  3. Hold the AGM

    Annual General Meeting must be held by 30 September (or by 31 December for first-year companies). Shareholders approve the accounts.

  4. File AOC-4 with attachments

    Login to MCA21 V3 portal → fill AOC-4 → attach financials, auditor's report, board report → DSC-sign by director and a professional (CA / CS / CMA) → pay fee → submit.

  5. File MGT-7 within 60 days of AGM

    Similar process — fill MGT-7 / 7A with shareholding and director details → DSC-sign → submit.

ROC filings are not optional. The compliance cost feels annoying — until you see a director-disqualification order, which is the cost of skipping them.

Common mistakes

⚠️
Mistake 1 — skipping the AGMNo AGM means you can't file AOC-4 (since financials need shareholder approval). Many founders defer AGM and the cascading delays compound.
⚠️
Mistake 2 — Form ADT-1 forgottenAuditor re-appointment via ADT-1 must be filed within 15 days of AGM. People often forget — and the company is technically without a statutory auditor.
⚠️
Mistake 3 — DIR-3 KYC missedEvery director must file annual KYC (DIR-3 KYC) by 30 September. Missing it deactivates your DIN — and you cannot sign any other MCA forms until it's revived (₹5,000 fee).
