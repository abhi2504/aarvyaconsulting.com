ITR for senior citizens & pensioners

Higher exemption, special deductions, advance tax relief. Senior citizens have several tax advantages — here they are.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Senior (60–79): basic exemption ₹3L (old regime). Super senior (80+): ₹5L.
  • 80TTB — ₹50,000 deduction on interest from savings, FD, RD, bonds (vs ₹10K under 80TTA).
  • Pension is taxed as "Salary" income. Standard deduction ₹50K (₹75K under new regime) applies.
  • Section 207 — seniors without business income exempt from advance tax. Just pay self-assessment by 31 July.

Higher basic exemption (old regime)

Age (as on 31 Mar)Basic exemption (old)Basic exemption (new)
Under 60₹2,50,000₹3,00,000
60–79 (Senior)₹3,00,000₹3,00,000

Note: New regime treats all ages equally. So old regime is often better for seniors.

Pension — how it's taxed

Two types:

  • Uncommuted (regular monthly) pension — Taxable as Salary. Standard deduction applies. Tax slabs apply.
  • Commuted (lump-sum) pension — Government employees: fully exempt. Non-government: 1/3rd exempt if you received gratuity, else 1/2 exempt.

Section 80TTB — ₹50,000 on interest

Most powerful deduction for seniors. Covers interest from:

  • Savings bank account
  • Fixed deposits (bank or post office)
  • Recurring deposits
  • Bonds, NCDs (interest portion)

Limit: ₹50,000/year. Old regime only. Replaces the ₹10K 80TTA which applies to under-60.

Advance tax exemption (Section 207)

Resident individuals aged 60+ without business or professional income are exempt from paying advance tax. They can pay full tax as self-assessment at filing time.

Health insurance — Section 80D

  • Self + spouse (senior): ₹50,000
  • Parents (senior): ₹50,000 additional
  • Total possible: ₹1,00,000
  • Includes premium paid + medical expenses for super-senior parents (with no insurance)

Form 15H — to avoid TDS

If your total income is below taxable limit, submit Form 15H (senior version of 15G) to your bank in April each year. Bank stops deducting TDS on interest.

ITR form for pensioners

  • ITR-1 (Sahaj) — pension + 1 house + other sources, total income up to ₹50L. Most pensioners.
  • ITR-2 — if you have capital gains, more than 1 house, foreign assets
  • ITR-3 — if you also have business/professional income

For a senior couple, ₹6 lakh of FD interest can be tax-free with smart use of ₹3L basic exemption + 80TTB. Many seniors overpay simply because they don't claim what they're entitled to.
