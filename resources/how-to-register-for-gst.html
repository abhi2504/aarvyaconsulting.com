How to register for GST — step by step (free, online, ~7 days)

Government fee: ₹0. Time: 5–10 working days. Here's the entire process from form fill to GSTIN.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Apply at gst.gov.in → Services → Registration → New Registration.
  • Government fee: ₹0. Anyone charging "GST registration fee" is a middleman.
  • Documents: PAN, Aadhaar, business proof, photo, bank details, address proof + NOC.
  • Timeline: ARN in minutes; GSTIN in 5–10 working days (Aadhaar-authenticated is fastest).

Before you start

Make sure you actually need GST first. Read Do you need GST registration? for the 3-question test. Voluntary registration is fine too, but make sure you understand the monthly filing obligations.

What you'll need

  • PAN of business / proprietor
  • Aadhaar of authorised signatory (linked to mobile)
  • Proof of business — partnership deed / certificate of incorporation / proprietorship declaration
  • Passport-size photograph (JPG, <100 KB)
  • Bank account proof — cancelled cheque or first page of passbook or bank statement
  • Principal place of business — recent electricity bill / property tax receipt / rent agreement
  • NOC (if rented or in another's name)
  • Authorisation letter for the authorised signatory (if not the proprietor)

The 2-part application

Step-by-step

  1. Part A — Get a TRN (Temporary Reference Number)

    Visit gst.gov.in → Services → Registration → New Registration. Pick "Taxpayer", state, district, PAN, business name, email, mobile. Submit. You'll get OTPs on email + mobile.

    After OTP validation you receive a TRN (Temporary Reference Number). It's valid for 15 days.

  2. Part B — Fill business details

    Login with TRN → My Saved Application → continue. Fill 10 sections:

    • Business Details: trade name, constitution, reason for registration, date
    • Promoters / Partners: details + photos + DIN (if applicable)
    • Authorised Signatory: who'll sign on behalf
    • Authorised Representative: if you have a CA / tax pro
    • Principal Place of Business: with proof + NOC
    • Additional Places: branches, godowns
    • Goods & Services: top 5 HSN/SAC codes
    • Bank Account: details + proof (can be added post-registration too)
    • State-specific information
    • Verification: submit with Aadhaar OTP / EVC / DSC

  3. Aadhaar authentication (recommended)

    You get a pop-up offering Aadhaar authentication. Choose Yes. It speeds up approval (often 3 working days vs 10). Without it, physical verification of premises may be triggered.

  4. Submit & receive ARN

    On submission you get an ARN (Application Reference Number) — your tracking ID. Save it.

  5. Wait for the department to review

    Typical outcomes within 5–10 working days:

    • Approval: GSTIN issued. Email + SMS with login credentials. Done!
    • Query (Form REG-03): department wants clarification — respond via Form REG-04 within 7 working days.
    • Rejection (Form REG-05): rare for genuine businesses; usually means documents inadequate.

  6. Activate the GSTIN

    Login at gst.gov.in with your new GSTIN. Update profile, add bank account if not done. Start filing returns from the next applicable period.

Common rejection reasons

⚠️
Top 5 rejection reasons1. Address proof name doesn't match electricity bill name (no NOC). 2. Blurry / cropped photo of business premises. 3. PAN name vs business name mismatch on application. 4. NOC missing signatures or unstamped. 5. Bank proof in different name from PAN holder.
