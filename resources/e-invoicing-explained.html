E-invoicing under GST — who needs it and how

Not "email an invoice". E-invoicing means registering each B2B invoice with the government before sending it.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Currently mandatory for taxpayers with aggregate turnover > ₹5 crore (from 1 Aug 2023).
  • Each B2B invoice must be reported to the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) to get an IRN + QR code.
  • Applies to B2B, exports, RCM supplies — not to B2C below ₹2.5L or composition dealers.
  • Invoice without valid IRN is invalid → buyer can't claim ITC.

What is e-invoicing exactly?

Before sending an invoice to your buyer, you upload its data (in a standard JSON format) to the government's IRP. The IRP:

  • Validates the data
  • Issues a unique 64-character Invoice Reference Number (IRN)
  • Generates a QR code embedding key invoice data
  • Sends the data to GSTN (auto-populates GSTR-1) and to NIC e-way bill portal

You then print the invoice with the IRN and QR code on it and send to the buyer.

Who needs to e-invoice (turnover thresholds)

ThresholdEffective from
> ₹500 cr1 Oct 2020
> ₹100 cr1 Jan 2021
> ₹50 cr1 Apr 2021
> ₹20 cr1 Apr 2022
> ₹10 cr1 Oct 2022
> ₹5 cr1 Aug 2023 (current)

"Turnover" = aggregate turnover in any preceding FY since 2017-18. Once you cross, you stay covered.

What's NOT covered

  • SEZ developers (SEZ units are covered)
  • Insurance, banking, financial institutions, NBFCs
  • Goods Transport Agency (GTA)
  • Passenger transportation services
  • Multiplex / cinema ticket suppliers
  • B2C invoices below ₹2.5L (B2C above ₹2.5L still need only QR, not IRN)
  • Composition dealers

How to generate an e-invoice

  1. Get onboarded on the IRP

    Visit einvoice1.gst.gov.in → register your GSTIN. Set up API credentials if integrating via ERP/software.

  2. Pick a method

    3 ways:

    • Direct API integration from your ERP / accounting software — best for high volume
    • GSP / ASP service — third-party gateway (e.g., ClearTax, Masters India, IRIS)
    • Bulk upload — on the IRP portal manually (good for < 100 invoices/month)

  3. Prepare invoice in standard schema

    The e-invoice schema (Schema v1.1) specifies ~140 fields, of which ~30 are mandatory. Your software handles mapping.

  4. Upload & receive IRN

    Send JSON to IRP. Get back IRN + QR code (instantaneous, milliseconds).

  5. Include IRN/QR on the invoice

    Print the invoice with IRN (64 chars) and QR code visible. Send to buyer.

  6. Verify GSTR-1 auto-population

    By default, every e-invoiced invoice flows to your GSTR-1. Verify before filing — sometimes credit notes or amendments don't flow correctly.

Common mistakes

⚠️
Mistake — issuing invoice before getting IRNAn invoice without IRN (when you're covered) is legally invalid. Buyer loses ITC. Always generate IRN first, then print + dispatch.
⚠️
Mistake — wrong HSN / GSTIN of buyerIRP rejects. You get an error. Fix the source data — don't use a different invoice number.
💡
Pro tipCancel-and-reissue window is 24 hours from IRN generation. After that, only credit notes / debit notes can correct.

E-invoicing isn't extra work — it's auto-data-entry into GSTR-1, GSTR-2B and e-way bill. Once set up right, it saves time. The pain is the setup, not the daily use.
