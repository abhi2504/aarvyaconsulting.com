How to respond to an Income Tax notice

Most IT notices today are auto-generated. Most can be closed in minutes with the right response. Here's how.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Don't ignore. Every notice has a deadline. Missing it leads to adverse orders.
  • Read the section number — it tells you what's wanted.
  • Respond on the IT portal under Pending Actions → e-Proceedings / Compliance Portal.
  • Most common — 143(1) intimation (auto-adjustment) — usually just verify and proceed.

Step-by-step response

  1. Find the notice on the portal

    Login at incometax.gov.in → Pending Actions → e-Proceedings or Compliance Portal.

  2. Read carefully

    Note: section, AY, issue, document reference, deadline, response form.

  3. Verify the underlying data

    Often the issue is a mismatch — your ITR vs AIS / Form 26AS / TIS. Pull both. Compare.

  4. Decide your response

    Options:

    • Agree — pay the demand / accept the adjustment
    • Partially agree — pay what's correct, respond explaining the rest
    • Disagree — submit point-by-point reply with supporting docs

  5. Submit reply on the portal

    Most notices have a "Submit Response" button. Attach PDF support docs. Use the appropriate response category.

  6. Track status

    Officer review. May raise further questions, close case, or proceed to assessment.

The most common notice — 143(1) intimation

After your ITR is processed, IT sends a 143(1) email. Possible outcomes:

  • No demand / No refund — your ITR was accepted as filed. No action.
  • Refund — confirms refund being credited. No action.
  • Demand — they've adjusted something (e.g., TDS not matching, deduction disallowed). Read carefully.

If you disagree with the adjustment, file a rectification request under Section 154 on the portal.

e-Verification scheme — the new common case

The AIS vs ITR mismatch system flags differences and asks you to confirm / explain. Common triggers:

  • Capital gain on shares from broker that you didn't report
  • Bank interest above 26AS that you missed
  • Cash deposit pattern in your account
  • Property purchase / sale flag
  • Foreign remittance

Login → Compliance Portal → respond per transaction. Either confirm + amend ITR (via revised return / ITR-U), or explain why no income.

⚠️
Don't blindly accept errorsSometimes AIS shows your spouse's bank interest under your PAN by mistake. Or a property gift considered as sale. Take time to verify before agreeing to any demand.

Most income-tax notices today are not allegations. They're automated checks. Polite, factual responses with supporting documents close 90% in one round.
