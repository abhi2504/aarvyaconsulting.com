Udyam / MSME registration — free, 10 minutes, no middleman

Every Indian small business should have this number. Don't pay ₹999 for what the government gives away for ₹0.

Priya · 28 · Freelance designer (back again)

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Only one official portal: udyamregistration.gov.in. Government fee: ₹0.
  • You need: Aadhaar (linked to mobile) + PAN + GSTIN (if applicable). Done.
  • Benefits — cheaper loans, 50% rebate on patent fees, 45-day payment law protection, eligibility for govt tenders.
  • Avoid any URL that ends in .org / .in / .net. The official one is .gov.in.

What is MSME / Udyam, in plain English

MSME = Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise. The Government of India offers a basket of benefits to businesses in this category — lower-rate loans, protection from delayed payments, reserved tenders, patent fee rebates.

To get those benefits, you need to be officially classified. That classification happens via Udyam Registration — a free 10-minute online process. The certificate is your URN (Udyam Registration Number).

Are you actually an MSME?

The classification is by both investment in plant & machinery AND turnover. The higher one wins.

Micro

Most freelancers / shops

Investment ≤ ₹1 cr Turnover ≤ ₹5 cr

Where the vast majority sit. Maximum benefit basket per scheme.

Small

Growing trade / service

Investment ≤ ₹10 cr Turnover ≤ ₹50 cr

Still in MSME bracket. Almost identical benefits to Micro.

Medium

Mid-market

Investment ≤ ₹50 cr Turnover ≤ ₹250 cr

Last tier in MSME. Above this you're "large" — no scheme benefits.

Why bother registering?

The big ones, in the order they actually matter:

👉 Faster payments from buyers. Under the MSMED Act, any buyer must pay an MSME supplier within 45 days. Beyond that, compound interest at 3× bank rate kicks in. Enterprises take this seriously — it changes how your invoices get prioritised.
👉 Priority sector lending. Banks offer loans at lower rates (often 1–2% below market) to MSMEs.
👉 Government tenders. Many departments reserve 25%+ of procurement for MSMEs. Some tenders are MSME-exclusive.
👉 Credit guarantee schemes (CGTMSE), interest subvention, technology upgradation grants — gateway is your Udyam.
👉 Patent / trademark filings — 50% rebate on application fees.
👉 Easier downstream registrations — many government schemes (Udyam Sakhi, PMEGP, etc.) need Udyam as the prerequisite.

The 6-step registration

1
Open the only real portal

udyamregistration.gov.in. Bookmark it. Any other URL is a middleman.

2
Click "For New Entrepreneurs"

If you held an old UAM, pick "For those having registration as EM-II or UAM" instead.

3
Aadhaar + OTP

Aadhaar of the proprietor / managing partner / signatory. Must be linked to a mobile that can receive OTP.

4
PAN + business details

Type of organisation, location, NIC code(s) for your activity, employee count, investment, turnover, bank account.

5
Submit

System auto-classifies you as Micro / Small / Medium based on the values you entered. Done in under a minute.

6
Download your URN certificate

Issued instantly. Format: UDYAM-XX-00-0001234. Save the PDF. Add it to your invoice footer.

The ₹999 scam — how to spot it

⚠️ The middleman pattern

Search "Udyam registration" on Google and the top results will be paid ads from sites like udyam-registration.org, udyam-portal.in, udyamcertificate.in. They charge ₹499 to ₹1,999. They just submit your info on the official free portal and email you the same PDF you'd get for free. The only legitimate URL is udyamregistration.gov.in. Anything else is a markup.

What you'll need to keep handy

  • Aadhaar number of the proprietor / partner / signatory
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile (for OTP)
  • PAN of the business or proprietor
  • GSTIN (if you already have one — mandatory only if GST-liable)
  • NIC code for your business activity (search the official NIC list)
  • Bank account number + IFSC
  • Investment + turnover figures (approximate is fine; system reclassifies annually from your ITR / GST data)

Udyam registration is free. Anyone charging you for it is selling air. Same form, same portal, same certificate — for ₹0.

— Priya, after dodging the ₹999 ad

What if you don't have Aadhaar / PAN?

If you run an informal micro enterprise without Aadhaar or PAN, the Udyam Assist Platform exists to onboard you through specified intermediary agencies and SIDBI. Walk into a District Industries Centre or a SIDBI branch and ask.

What changes after registration?

The day after you have a URN:

👉 Add "MSME Registered · Udyam URN: UDYAM-XX-...." to your invoice footer. Buyers notice.
👉 When sending invoices to enterprises, attach a one-line MSMED reminder ("payment due within 45 days as per MSMED Act"). It changes how invoices get prioritised.
👉 Bookmark champions.gov.in — the MSME grievance portal. Use it if a buyer doesn't pay within 45 days.
👉 Update Udyam annually with new turnover / investment figures (auto-pulls from ITR + GST data; you just confirm).
