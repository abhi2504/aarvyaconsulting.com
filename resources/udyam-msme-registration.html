Udyam / MSME registration — free, online, 10 minutes

Every Indian small business should have this. It's free. It takes 10 minutes. Don't fall for the ₹999 "registration" scam sites.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Only one official portal: udyamregistration.gov.in. Zero rupee government fee.
  • You need: Aadhaar (linked to mobile) + PAN + GSTIN (if applicable). That's it.
  • Benefits: Cheaper loans (priority sector), 50% rebate on patent fees, 50-day payment law protection from buyers, eligibility for government tenders.
  • Avoid: Any site that charges you. They're middlemen using the official free portal anyway.

What is MSME / Udyam?

MSME = Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise. The Government of India offers special benefits to businesses in this category. To claim those benefits, you need to be officially classified — that's done via the Udyam Registration.

The size definitions

CategoryInvestment in plant & machineryAnnual turnover
MicroUp to ₹1 croreUp to ₹5 crore
SmallUp to ₹10 croreUp to ₹50 crore
MediumUp to ₹50 croreUp to ₹250 crore

Both criteria apply — investment AND turnover. The higher one decides your category. Source: msme.gov.in.

Why bother registering?

  • Faster payments from buyers — MSMED Act says any buyer must pay an MSME supplier within 45 days. Beyond that, compound interest at 3× bank rate. Big enterprises take this seriously.
  • Priority sector lending — banks offer loans at lower rates (often 1–2% below market) to MSMEs.
  • Subsidies and schemes — credit guarantee schemes (CGTMSE), interest subvention, technology upgradation grants.
  • Government tenders — many gov departments reserve 25%+ of procurement for MSMEs. Some tenders are MSME-exclusive.
  • Tax benefits — exemption from direct tax laws, lower stamp duty in some states.
  • Patent / trademark rebate — 50% rebate on application fees.
  • Easier registrations elsewhere — many government schemes (Udyam Sakhi, PMEGP, etc.) need MSME registration as the gateway.

Step-by-step registration

  1. Open the official portal

    Visit udyamregistration.gov.in. Bookmark it. If a site asks you to "pay ₹499 / ₹999 / ₹1,499 for Udyam registration" — close that tab. It's not official.

  2. Click "For New Entrepreneurs"

    Or, if you already have UAM (the old system), pick "For those having registration as EM-II or UAM".

  3. Enter Aadhaar number

    The Aadhaar must be of the proprietor / managing partner / authorised signatory. Aadhaar must be linked to your mobile (you'll get OTP).

  4. Validate OTP

    OTP comes to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Enter it. Aadhaar gets validated.

  5. Enter PAN and business details

    PAN of the business (or proprietor for sole prop). Type of organisation, location, NIC code(s) (industry classification), employees, investment, turnover, bank account.

  6. Submit

    System auto-classifies you as Micro/Small/Medium based on values. You get a Udyam Registration Number and a downloadable e-certificate immediately.

Watch out — paid "Udyam" sitesSearch "Udyam registration" on Google and you'll see 20 ads for paid services with names like "udyam-registration.org" or "udyam-portal.in". These are middlemen. They just submit your info on the official free portal and pocket the fee. The only government portal is udyamregistration.gov.in — check the URL.

What about Udyam Assist?If you don't have an Aadhaar / PAN, the Udyam Assist Platform helps onboard informal micro enterprises. Run via specified intermediary agencies and SIDBI.

Documents / info you'll need

  • Aadhaar number of proprietor / managing partner / signatory
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP)
  • PAN of the business or proprietor
  • GSTIN (if you have one)
  • NIC code of your business activity (search at NIC code search)
  • Bank account details (account number + IFSC)
  • Investment in plant & machinery + turnover figures (approx)

Udyam registration is free. Anyone charging you for it is selling air. The same form, the same portal — for ₹0.
