Hiring your first employee — payroll basics

Offer letter, salary breakup, PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, Form 16. From day one. Set it up right and you won't redo it.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • PF mandatory when you cross 20 employees (some industries — 10). Optional below.
  • ESI applicable on employees earning ≤ ₹21,000/month gross, once you cross 10 employees in covered states.
  • Professional tax — state-level, ~₹2,500/year max in most states.
  • TDS on salary — mandatory if taxable income exceeds basic exemption.
  • Even 1 employee = offer letter, salary slip, signed appointment, payroll record.

Before you hire

  • Have your business registered (Pvt Ltd / LLP / proprietorship — see our guide)
  • Have a current account in the business name
  • Get a TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number) — needed before you cut TDS
  • Decide on a payroll software (Tally, Zoho Payroll, RazorpayX Payroll, GreytHR, etc.) or outsource

The salary structure (CTC → in-hand)

For a ₹6L/year CTC employee, a clean structure looks like:

ComponentMonthly (₹)Notes
Basic (40% of CTC)20,000Triggers PF/ESI/gratuity
HRA (50% of basic)10,000Tax-exempt up to actual rent rules
Special / Other allowances17,500Taxable
Employer PF (12% of basic)2,400Part of CTC, not in hand
Total CTC~50,000≈ ₹6L/year

From the in-hand: deduct employee PF (12% of basic), professional tax, and any TDS. The net is what hits the bank.

What is PF (Provident Fund)?

Retirement savings scheme run by EPFO. Both you and the employee contribute 12% of basic salary monthly.

  • Mandatory once you cross 20 employees (10 in some industries)
  • Voluntary registration possible from any size — improves credibility with employees
  • EPFO portal: epfindia.gov.in
  • Monthly ECR (Electronic Challan-cum-Return) due by 15th

What is ESI (Employees' State Insurance)?

Healthcare and short-term disability scheme for low-income employees. Applicable on employees earning ≤ ₹21,000/month gross.

  • Employee contributes 0.75%, employer contributes 3.25% of gross salary
  • Mandatory when you cross 10 employees in ESI-covered areas
  • ESIC portal: esic.gov.in
  • Monthly payment by 15th, half-yearly return

Professional Tax

State-level tax on salaried/professional income. Different rates per state.

Employer registers, deducts from salary, deposits monthly/quarterly with the state. Salaried employee thresholds vary per state.

TDS on salary (Section 192)

If the employee's annual taxable income (after deductions) crosses the exemption limit, you (as employer) must deduct TDS every month and deposit it with the IT department.

  1. At year start — collect declaration

    Employee submits Form 12BB declaring investments, HRA, rent, home-loan interest, etc.

  2. Estimate annual tax

    Project annual income → apply slabs → divide by 12 → that's monthly TDS.

  3. Deduct & deposit monthly

    By 7th of next month. NSDL OLTAS challan.

  4. File quarterly TDS return

    Form 24Q every quarter — by 31 Jul, 31 Oct, 31 Jan, 31 May.

  5. Issue Form 16

    To each employee by 15 June following year-end. Shows full salary breakup + tax deducted.

Don't pay first employee from your personal account. Don't skip the offer letter. Don't promise a salary you can't pay PF on. The shortcut today becomes a Labour Department visit tomorrow.

The offer letter — what to include

  • Designation, start date, reporting manager
  • Probation period (if any) — 3 to 6 months typical
  • Working hours, office location (remote/hybrid)
  • CTC breakup (annexure with all components)
  • Leave policy
  • Notice period (30/60/90 days)
  • Confidentiality / IP assignment / non-compete clauses
  • Termination clause
