E-way bill — when you need it, how to make one

Moving goods worth over ₹50,000? You'll likely need an e-way bill. Here's everything in one place.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Required when goods worth > ₹50,000 are moved (inter-state by default; intra-state per state rules).
  • Generate at ewaybillgst.gov.in before transport starts.
  • Validity: 1 day per 200 km (or part) for normal vehicles; longer for ODC (over-dimensional cargo).
  • Carry e-way bill (printout or on phone) with invoice during transport.

When is it required?

  • Inter-state movement of goods worth > ₹50,000 — mandatory
  • Intra-state — depends on state (most states ≥ ₹50,000; some states like Delhi have ₹1 lakh; check your state)
  • Job work to inter-state job worker — regardless of value
  • Handicraft goods moved inter-state by exempt persons — regardless of value

When NOT required

  • Non-motorised transport (handcart, cycle)
  • Goods exempt from GST (e.g. fresh fruits/vegetables, raw silk, etc.)
  • Specified goods (Annexure to Rule 138 — alcohol, petroleum, etc.)
  • Distance < 50 km within same state for delivery
  • Movement within port / airport / cargo terminal for customs clearance

Who generates it?

  • The consignor (seller) — usually the default
  • The consignee (buyer) — if seller is unregistered
  • The transporter — if neither party generates and value > ₹50,000

How to generate

  1. Login to E-way bill portal

    Visit ewaybillgst.gov.in. Login with GSTIN and password.

  2. Click "Generate New" (Part A + B)

    Fill: Transaction type (Outward), Sub-type (Supply), Document type (Tax invoice), Doc No., Doc Date, From (you), To (buyer with GSTIN), Item details (HSN, qty, value, tax rate), Transport details.

  3. Enter vehicle / transporter details

    Vehicle number (for own transport) or Transporter ID + vehicle (for hired transport). For long-distance, multiple vehicles can be updated en route.

  4. Submit & print

    System generates a 12-digit EBN (E-way Bill Number). Print it or save the PDF. Carry during transport.

  5. Update vehicle changes en route

    If goods are trans-shipped, update Part B with new vehicle number before continuing.

Validity period

DistanceValidity
Up to 200 km1 day
Every additional 200 km (or part)+1 day
For ODC: up to 20 km1 day
ODC: every additional 20 km+1 day

Validity starts from when Part B is updated.

Penalties for non-compliance

  • Tax + 100% of tax due, OR ₹10,000 (whichever is higher)
  • Goods + conveyance may be detained until tax + penalty is paid
  • Repeat offences attract higher penalties
⚠️
Avoid thisMoving goods on Saturday evening because portal "might be down" — checkpost officers don't care. No e-way bill = penalty. Generate before dispatch, always.

SMS option (quick generate)

Register your mobile under "SMS" option in the portal. Then SMS-generate e-way bills using formats like:

EWBG TRAN_TYPE REC_GSTIN DELPLACE INVOICE INV_DATE TOTAL_VAL HSN APRX_DIS

Useful when portal is slow or you're on the road. Receive EBN by SMS.

Treat e-way bill like a passport for your goods. No goods leave the warehouse without one. Build it into your dispatch SOP.
