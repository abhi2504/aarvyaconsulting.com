Pvt Ltd vs LLP vs OPC — which structure is actually right for you?

Three structures, three philosophies, very different annual bills. Here's how to choose without regretting it 2 years later.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Pvt Ltd — the only one VCs and angels will fund. Highest compliance cost (~₹12k–₹20k/year). Best for serious growth.
  • LLP — limited liability + partnership flexibility. Lower compliance (~₹6k–₹10k/year). No equity fundraising. Best for services / consulting / family businesses.
  • OPC — Pvt Ltd-grade protection for a single founder. Slightly lower compliance than Pvt Ltd. Auto-converts to Pvt Ltd at ₹2 crore turnover or ₹50 lakh paid-up capital.

The honest framework

Most founders pick a structure based on what sounds prestigious. That's the wrong question. The right question is:

"What's my plan for the next 3 years?"

Plan to raise from investors? Pvt Ltd. Plan to stay bootstrapped with a partner? LLP. Solo for now, might grow later? OPC. Just testing? Proprietorship (and read our starting a company guide for that path).

When Pvt Ltd is the right call

  • You plan to raise from investors — angels, VCs, or even friends-and-family for equity
  • You want to issue ESOPs to early team members
  • Your business has product / IP / scalable revenue (SaaS, D2C, fintech)
  • You have 2+ co-founders who want clear ownership splits
  • You expect to do significant B2B deals with large companies — many enterprises only contract with private limited entities
🎯
Reality checkIf "we might raise someday" is the only Pvt Ltd reason — you can always convert later. Don't pay ₹15k/year in extra compliance for a hypothetical.

When LLP is the better call

  • You're a service business — consulting, design, law, accounting, agency work
  • You're running a family business with multiple partners
  • You don't need outside equity funding
  • You want limited liability without the compliance burden of a Pvt Ltd
  • You want partner-style profit sharing flexibility (LLP agreement can structure this)

When OPC fits

  • You're a solo founder, no co-founders likely in next 12–24 months
  • You want limited liability (don't want personal assets at risk)
  • You don't want partners but want a corporate structure for credibility / contracts
  • You're prepared to convert to Pvt Ltd if you cross ₹2 crore turnover or ₹50 lakh paid-up capital
⚠️
OPC limitationOnly an Indian citizen who's a resident in India can form an OPC. Foreign nationals and NRIs cannot. Also, a person can be a member of only one OPC at a time.

Pick the cheapest legal wrapper that fits your next 2 years — not the prestigious one that fits your dreams.

Real conversion paths

Yes, you can change structures later. Common conversions:

  • Proprietorship → Pvt Ltd / LLP — straightforward. You incorporate the new entity and transfer assets / liabilities.
  • OPC → Pvt Ltd — mandatory if you cross thresholds, voluntary anytime after 2 years.
  • LLP → Pvt Ltd — possible but procedurally heavy. Better to start as Pvt Ltd if you're sure about fundraising.
  • Partnership → LLP — common upgrade path. Direct conversion route available.
