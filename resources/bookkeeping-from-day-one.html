Bookkeeping from day one — why it saves you later

A WhatsApp screenshot of payments is not "books". Here's the simple discipline that saves 100 hours every March.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Track every rupee in and out from day one — even before you incorporate.
  • One business bank account separate from personal. No mixing.
  • Pick a tool early: Tally, Zoho Books or QuickBooks. Spreadsheet is fine for the first 3 months only.
  • Close books monthly. Reconcile bank. Tag categories. 30 minutes of discipline saves 30 hours at year-end.

Why founders skip bookkeeping (and regret it)

Most founders think "I'll figure out the books at year-end". Then March arrives. They have:

  • A bank statement with 700 entries
  • WhatsApp screenshots of "customer paid"
  • 30 expense vouchers in a drawer
  • Zero memory of what half of it was for

The CA spends 2 weeks trying to reconstruct this. The audit picks up errors. Your tax bill is higher than it should be because legitimate expenses got missed. Your loan application gets rejected because financials are unsigned.

The fix is 30 minutes a week from day one.

The 5 rules of clean bookkeeping

1. One business bank account. Always.

Open it the day you incorporate. Stop using your personal card for business expenses. Every founder who skipped this rule ended up paying a CA to untangle the mess.

2. Record every transaction within 7 days

Don't wait for month-end. Memory fades fast. By Friday evening, log everything that came in or went out that week. Tools make this 5 minutes.

3. Every expense needs a category and a description

"₹8,500 — vendor" is useless. "₹8,500 — Vendor: AWS Inc — Category: Hosting — Invoice #INV-2026-04-12" is gold. Searchable, audit-friendly, P&L-ready.

4. Reconcile bank monthly

Match every line in your bank statement to an entry in your books. If something doesn't match, you've found a bug. Fix it now, not in March.

5. Close the month within 7 days

By the 7th of every month, last month's books are locked. P&L generated. Bank reconciled. After that, you only add adjusting entries with notes.

The monthly close routine

  1. Download bank + credit-card statements

    For the previous month. Save to a "books" folder named by month.

  2. Match every line to a book entry

    Tally / Zoho have auto-reconciliation. Tick off each line. Unmatched = entry needs adding.

  3. Verify GST workings

    Output GST (sales) matches your invoices. Input GST (purchases) matches your purchase bills. Both should reconcile with GSTR-2B.

  4. Check TDS deductions

    Salary TDS deducted? Vendor TDS where applicable? Deposited by 7th?

  5. Generate P&L and balance sheet

    Review. Anomalies? Investigate. Numbers should answer "how much did I make, how much do I have, what do I owe".

  6. Lock the period

    Set lock date in your tool. No accidental backdated entries.

Bookkeeping isn't accounting. It's just keeping a clean diary of money. The skill isn't math — it's the discipline to update the diary every week.
