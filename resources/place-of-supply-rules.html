Place of supply — the rule that decides CGST/SGST vs IGST

Same invoice, wrong split. Most GST notices for small businesses start here.

R
Rohit · 29 · Freelance developer in Mumbai

Rohit's billed three clients last month. One in Mumbai (his state), one in Delhi, one in San Francisco. Each invoice needs different tax treatment — and most freelancers default-pick "CGST+SGST" on every invoice. "Wrong call," his CA said.

The fix is one rule: place of supply.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Place of supply = recipient's location (for most services / B2B goods).
  • Supplier and recipient in same state → CGST + SGST.
  • Different states OR export → IGST (zero for export with LUT).
  • Get this wrong = invoice rejected, buyer ITC blocked, your GSTR-1 reconciliation fails.

The simple rule (and the exceptions)

B2B
Services to registered businesses

Place of supply = location of recipient (their GSTIN's state). Section 12(2)(a).

B2C
Services to unregistered consumers

Place of supply = address on record. If no address, your (supplier's) state.

Goods
Goods supply

Place of supply = where movement terminates (final delivery point). Section 10.

Export
Cross-border

Place of supply outside India. Treated as inter-state → IGST 0% with LUT, or IGST + refund.

Rohit's three invoices, decoded

Mumbai client

Same state

Intra-state → CGST 9% + SGST 9%. Place of supply: Maharashtra.

Delhi client

Different state

Inter-state → IGST 18%. Place of supply: Delhi (state code 07).

SF client

Export of service

Outside India → IGST 0% with LUT. Place of supply code: 96.

Special place-of-supply rules (services)

Section 12 has 14 special rules for specific service types. The frequently-missed ones:

  • Immovable property (architects, engineers, real estate) → place where the property is located
  • Restaurant / catering / personal grooming → place where service is physically performed
  • Training / coaching → place where service is performed (in-person); B2B = recipient's state (online)
  • Admission to events / sports / culture → place where event is held
  • Transportation of goods → place where goods are handed over for transportation (registered recipient: recipient's state)
  • Passenger transportation → where the passenger embarks
  • Telecom / DTH / internet → location of subscriber (billing address)
  • Banking / financial services → location of recipient on record
  • OIDAR (cross-border digital) → location of recipient

The state codes you need

Maharashtra 27 · Karnataka 29 · Tamil Nadu 33 · Delhi 07 · Haryana 06 · Gujarat 24 · UP 09 · West Bengal 19 · Telangana 36 · Andhra Pradesh 37 · Kerala 32 · Rajasthan 08 · MP 23 · Punjab 03 · Other Country (export): 96. Wrong state code on invoice = wrong place of supply = mismatch in 2B → buyer ITC blocked.

The 3 mistakes that trigger notices

👉 Charging CGST+SGST when buyer is in another state. Money lands in your state's coffers; buyer's state shows zero credit; their ITC blocked. They'll chase you. Notice follows.
👉 Wrong state code on the e-invoice. Auto-flagged by IRP. Some types get cancelled before issue.
👉 Branch / additional place mixed up. If your registered HQ is Mumbai but you actually shipped from your Delhi branch, place of supply changes. Use the right GSTIN for the branch.

Place of supply for goods (Section 10)

For goods, the rule depends on whether movement is involved:

👉 Movement of goods (most cases) → place of supply is where movement terminates for delivery to recipient.
👉 No movement (e.g., asset on-site sale) → place where goods are located at time of delivery.
👉 Bill-to-ship-to → place is the location of the third-party recipient (not the bill-to). Critical for Amazon FBA, drop-shipping, distribution chains.
👉 Import → location of the importer.
👉 Export → place outside India.

The cardinal sin in B2B GST is wrong CGST/SGST/IGST split. The number's the same but the recipient state's ITC ledger says "missing". You'll be chased.

— Rohit's CA

Bill-to vs Ship-to — the e-commerce trap

You sell from Mumbai. Buyer's billing GSTIN is in Bengaluru. They ship to their Hyderabad warehouse. Which state's GST?

👉 Place of supply = ship-to state (Telangana). So IGST applies.
👉 But invoice still goes to Bengaluru GSTIN.
👉 On the GSTR-1, you report "Place of Supply: Telangana (36)" but "Recipient GSTIN: 29..." (Karnataka).

Common in marketplace selling. Most platforms auto-handle this; if you're billing direct, take care.

Quick answers

Recipient location = location of registered place of business = Mumbai (state on GSTIN). Bill CGST+SGST if you're also Maharashtra; IGST otherwise. Where they "operate" is irrelevant to GST.

If invoice value is under ₹2.5L for B2C inter-state, you can summarise (no specific recipient GSTIN needed). Above ₹2.5L inter-state, full invoice details required with the recipient's state.

Issue a credit note for the wrong invoice + new invoice with correct IGST. File PMT-09 to transfer money between heads in your Electronic Cash Ledger. Inform buyer so they can fix their ITC claim.

"96" (Other Country). Mention on invoice. Also LUT ARN if billing at 0% IGST.

SEZ is treated as "inter-state" regardless of physical location. Always IGST. Zero-rated under LUT or with refund route.

For the export case
LUT for exporters — bill at 0% IGST
