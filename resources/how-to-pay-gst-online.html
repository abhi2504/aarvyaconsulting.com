How to pay your GST online — every step

From challan generation to confirmation receipt, in about 10 minutes. Works for monthly, quarterly and one-time payments.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Login to gst.gov.in → Services → Payments → Create Challan
  • Enter tax/interest/fee amounts under CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess heads
  • Pick a payment mode (Net Banking is fastest; UPI works for amounts up to ₹1L; NEFT/RTGS for big payments)
  • Pay → download the challan receipt → keep it forever

Before you start

Have these ready:

  • Your GSTIN and login password
  • The exact tax amount you owe (from your GSTR-3B calculation)
  • Net banking access OR UPI app OR debit/credit card

Step-by-step

  1. Go to gst.gov.in and log in

    Visit gst.gov.in. Click Login (top right). Enter your GSTIN as username and your password. Solve the captcha. Hit Login.

  2. Navigate: Services → Payments → Create Challan

    On the dashboard, hover over the Services menu. Click Payments → Create Challan. You'll land on the challan creation page.

  3. Enter the amounts

    You'll see a grid with columns for CGST, SGST/UTGST, IGST, Cess and rows for Tax, Interest, Penalty, Fee, Other. Enter your computed amounts in the right cells.

    For a standard monthly payment of intra-state supplies: split the GST equally between CGST and SGST. For inter-state supplies, the full amount goes under IGST.

  4. Choose payment mode

    You'll see four options:

    • E-Payment — Net Banking from authorised banks. Instant. Best for most.
    • Over the Counter (OTC) — only for challans up to ₹10,000. Pay at any authorised bank branch by cash/cheque/DD.
    • NEFT / RTGS — generate a Mandate Form, take to any bank. Useful for very large payments. Takes 1 working day.
    • Credit/Debit Card or UPI — available on the e-payment screen. UPI works up to ₹1 lakh per transaction.

  5. Generate the challan and pay

    Click Generate Challan. The system creates a CPIN (Common Portal Identification Number) — your unique payment reference. Challan is valid for 15 days.

    If you chose Net Banking — you're redirected to your bank's site to complete payment. UPI / Card — enter details and confirm. NEFT/RTGS — take the mandate form to your bank.

  6. Download the receipt

    Once payment succeeds, the GST portal updates your Electronic Cash Ledger and generates a payment receipt. Download it. Save it in your GST file. This is your proof of payment.

Common mistakes to avoid

⚠️
Wrong head allocationPutting IGST amount under CGST/SGST (or vice versa) is the #1 mistake. The money sits in the wrong ledger and your GSTR-3B won't reconcile. Always double-check the place of supply before allocating.
⚠️
Paying past CPIN expiryChallans expire in 15 days. If you start a challan and pay 16 days later, the payment fails. Generate fresh.
💡
Pro tipPay GST on or before the 18th of every month (3 days before the 20th deadline). This avoids last-day portal slowdowns and bank delays that can cause late-fee headaches.
