Capital gains tax — shares, MF, crypto, property

You sold something. You made money. Now the government wants its cut. Here's how much, and how to legally minimise it.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • STCG (Short-Term Capital Gain) — sold within holding limit. Higher tax rate.
  • LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gain) — sold after holding limit. Lower tax rate.
  • Equity shares / equity MFs: holding limit 12 months. STCG 15%, LTCG 10% above ₹1L.
  • Property / debt MF / gold: 24-36 months. STCG slab rate, LTCG 20% with indexation (for property; new rules for debt MFs).
  • Crypto / VDAs: flat 30% regardless of period. No expense deduction. 1% TDS on transfer.

How to compute gain

Basic formula: Gain = Sale price − Cost of acquisition − Cost of improvement − Selling expenses

For LTCG with indexation: Cost is adjusted upward by inflation (CII — Cost Inflation Index) before subtracting.

Indexation example — house bought ₹40L in 2010, sold ₹1.2 cr in 2025

CII 2010-11: 167. CII 2025-26: ~363 (illustrative).

Indexed cost = 40,00,000 × 363/167 ≈ ₹86,90,000

LTCG = 1,20,00,000 − 86,90,000 = ₹33,10,000

Tax at 20% = ₹6,62,000 (plus cess)

Equity-specific rules

LTCG ₹1 lakh exemption (Section 112A)

First ₹1 lakh of LTCG on listed equity / equity MFs in a year is exempt. Tax only on excess at 10%.

Grandfathering (Jan 31, 2018)

For equity held before 31 Jan 2018: cost is higher of:

  • Actual purchase cost, OR
  • Fair Market Value (highest quoted price) on 31 Jan 2018, but not more than sale price

This grandfathers pre-2018 gains, so only post-2018 appreciation is taxed.

Crypto / VDA — the strict regime (since FY 22-23)

  • Flat 30% tax + 4% cess (effective 31.2%)
  • No deduction except cost of acquisition
  • No set-off of losses against any other income (not even against other crypto gains in some interpretations — clarification ongoing)
  • Losses cannot be carried forward
  • 1% TDS on every transfer above threshold (₹50K/year for individuals, ₹10K for businesses)

Real estate — exemptions to save tax

Section 54 — reinvest in another residential property

  • Sold a residential property, reinvest LTCG in another residential property within 2 years (purchase) or 3 years (construction)
  • New property must be in India
  • Can claim exemption up to ₹10 cr (recent restriction from FY 23-24)

Section 54F — reinvest LTCG from other capital assets in residential property

For LTCG on non-residential assets (shares, gold, etc.) — invest net sale consideration (not just gain) in one residential property in India.

Section 54EC — invest in specified bonds

  • Invest LTCG (only from land/building) in NHAI / REC / PFC / IRFC bonds within 6 months
  • Max investment: ₹50 lakh per FY
  • Lock-in 5 years
  • Interest rate ~5%

The difference between a tax-aware investor and a careless one isn't returns — it's net-of-tax returns. Hold for 12 months, harvest ₹1L losses to offset gains, use 54EC bonds for real estate windfalls.
