Section 80C to 80U — every deduction explained

Under the old regime, smart use of Chapter VI-A deductions can drop your tax by ₹1–4 lakh. Here's every section that matters.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • 80C — ₹1.5 lakh limit (PPF, ELSS, EPF, LIC, principal of home loan, tuition fees…)
  • 80D — ₹25K self + ₹25K parents (₹50K if senior). Health insurance + preventive check-up.
  • 80CCD(1B) — additional ₹50K for NPS over 80C
  • 80G — donations to approved entities. 50% or 100% deduction.
  • None of these are available under the New Tax Regime (except 80CCD(2) employer NPS).

Section 80C — the ₹1.5L workhorse

The most-used section. Limit: ₹1,50,000 aggregated across all eligible investments & expenses.

Section 80CCD — NPS investments

  • 80CCD(1) — your own NPS contribution. Part of overall ₹1.5L (combined with 80C).
  • 80CCD(1B) — additional ₹50,000 for NPS, over and above the ₹1.5L limit. Total possible: ₹2L.
  • 80CCD(2) — employer's NPS contribution to your account. Up to 10% of salary (14% for govt employees). Also allowed under New Regime.

Section 80E — education loan interest

Full interest deduction (no upper cap) on loan taken for higher education — yours, spouse's, or children's.

Deduction available for 8 years from the year repayment starts.

Section 80EE / 80EEA — home loan interest (additional)

  • 80EE — additional ₹50K interest on first home loan (sanctioned FY 16-17), property value < ₹50L, loan < ₹35L
  • 80EEA — additional ₹1.5L on affordable home loan (sanctioned FY 19-20 to 21-22), stamp value ≤ ₹45L

These are over and above the ₹2L Section 24(b) interest deduction.

Section 80G — donations

  • 100% deduction without limit — donations to PM National Relief Fund, PM CARES, etc.
  • 50% deduction without limit — PM's Drought Relief, Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Fund
  • 100% deduction with 10%-of-AGI limit — Indian Olympic Association, etc.
  • 50% deduction with 10%-of-AGI limit — most NGOs registered under 12A/12AA

Cash donations > ₹2,000 are NOT eligible. Use UPI / cheque / bank transfer.

Section 80GG — rent (no HRA)

For salaried without HRA (or self-employed) paying rent. Least of:

  • ₹5,000/month
  • 25% of total income
  • Rent paid minus 10% of total income

Section 80TTA / 80TTB — savings interest

  • 80TTA — ₹10,000 on savings bank interest (under 60)
  • 80TTB — ₹50,000 on interest from savings + FD + RD + bonds (senior citizens 60+)

Section 80U — disability

  • Disabled person (40-80% disability) — ₹75,000
  • Severe disability (80%+) — ₹1,25,000

Need certificate from medical authority. Self-attested.

The Old Regime rewards documentation. Every receipt, premium, donation slip is potential ₹. The New Regime trades all this for lower slabs. Pick by your situation, not by what's easier.
