LUT for exporters — export without paying IGST upfront

If you export goods or services, the LUT is the most important annual GST filing you'll do. Here's why and how.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Exports are zero-rated under GST. Two options: pay IGST and claim refund, or file LUT and export without paying IGST.
  • LUT route is preferred — no cash blocked, no refund wait.
  • LUT is valid for one financial year. Renew every April.
  • File online on gst.gov.in → Services → User Services → Furnish Letter of Undertaking.

The 2 options for exporters

OptionWhat happensProsCons
Pay IGST, claim refundYou pay IGST on exports, then file Form RFD-01 to get it backSimple flowCash blocked for 30–90 days
Export under LUT (no IGST)You commit to no IGST payment via undertakingNo cash blocked. Faster.Annual renewal required

Who can file LUT?

Almost any registered taxpayer engaged in:

  • Export of goods or services
  • Supply of goods/services to SEZ developers / SEZ units

Exception: taxpayers prosecuted for offences exceeding ₹2.5 cr (in some specific cases) need a bond + bank guarantee instead.

How to file LUT (online)

  1. Login to gst.gov.in

    With your GSTIN and password.

  2. Navigate to LUT

    Services → User Services → Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT).

  3. Pick the FY

    Choose the financial year for which LUT is being filed (e.g. FY 2025-26).

  4. Fill the form (RFD-11)

    Most fields auto-populate. You need:

    • Confirm legal name, GSTIN
    • Two independent witnesses with address (any 2 individuals — not employees)
    • Acceptance of declaration clauses

  5. Sign with DSC / EVC

    For Companies / LLPs — DSC mandatory. For others — EVC (Aadhaar OTP) works.

  6. Receive Acknowledgement (ARN)

    Generated instantly. LUT is approved automatically — no officer intervention. Save the ARN + PDF.

What you commit to via LUT

  • To complete the export within 3 months of invoice date (goods) or within 1 year (services receipt)
  • To pay IGST + 18% interest if you fail to meet the export commitment
  • To follow all GST law provisions on exports

Common scenarios

Software services exported to a US client

File LUT. Invoice with "Export of services under LUT". No IGST charged. Receive payment in foreign currency. Conditions for "export of services":

  • Supplier in India
  • Recipient outside India
  • Place of supply outside India
  • Payment received in convertible foreign exchange (or INR allowed by RBI)
  • Supplier and recipient not establishments of same person

Goods exported via shipping

File LUT. Generate e-way bill if applicable. Tax invoice with shipping bill ref. Recover ITC on inputs through GST refund Form RFD-01.

Supply to SEZ

Treated as zero-rated. LUT applies. Same flow.

⚠️
Don't forget the annual renewalLUT is for ONE financial year. File the new one early April. Exporting after 31 March without renewed LUT means IGST must be paid on every invoice — claim refund later (cash flow hit).

LUT is a 10-minute annual task that saves you 90 days of working capital. Don't skip the renewal.
