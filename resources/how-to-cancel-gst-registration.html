How to cancel GST registration — step by step

Business closed, fallen below threshold, or just want to exit GST? Here's the right way to surrender your GSTIN cleanly.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • File Form REG-16 on gst.gov.in. Free.
  • You must have filed all pending returns up to date.
  • Pay reversal of ITC on closing stock (Section 29).
  • After cancellation, file Form GSTR-10 (final return) within 3 months.

When you can cancel

Common valid reasons:

  • Business has been discontinued / closed
  • Business transferred / merged / demerged
  • Change in constitution (e.g. proprietorship → Pvt Ltd) — surrender old GSTIN
  • Turnover fell below threshold permanently (and you don't have voluntary registration)
  • Other valid reasons (e.g. shifting state — old state GSTIN cancelled, new state GSTIN obtained)

Before you start — pre-requisites

  • All GSTR-1, GSTR-3B (or CMP-08 for composition) filed up to date
  • All tax + interest + late fees + penalty paid
  • Closing stock valuation done (raw materials, semi-finished, finished, capital goods)
  • ITC on closing stock computed for reversal
  • Bank account closed or migrated if relevant

Step-by-step

  1. Login to gst.gov.in

    With your GSTIN credentials.

  2. Navigate to cancellation

    Services → Registration → Application for Cancellation of Registration.

  3. Fill Form REG-16

    Sections:

    • Reason for cancellation (dropdown)
    • Date from which cancellation is requested
    • Tax payable on stock (closing balance)
    • Particulars of authorised signatory

  4. Closing stock & ITC reversal calculation

    Two methods (whichever is higher applies):

    • Input on stock × applicable rate
    • OR ITC availed earlier × (remaining life of asset / total life), for capital goods

  5. Submit with DSC / EVC

    You get an ARN. Application goes to officer.

  6. Officer review (within 30 days)

    Officer may approve via Form REG-19 OR raise queries via REG-17 — respond within 7 days.

  7. Cancellation order (REG-19)

    Effective date as per officer's order. GSTIN status changes to "Cancelled".

  8. File GSTR-10 (Final Return)

    Within 3 months of cancellation order. This is mandatory. Includes details of closing stock and ITC reversed.

Department-initiated cancellation (suo moto)

The department can cancel your GSTIN suo moto if:

  • You've not filed returns for 6 consecutive months (3 for composition)
  • Voluntary registration but not commenced business within 6 months
  • Fraudulent registration / misuse of GSTIN
  • Other contraventions

You'll receive Form REG-17 (Show Cause). Respond via REG-18 within 7 days. If unresolved, REG-19 cancellation order issues.

Revocation of cancellation

If GSTIN was cancelled by the department and you want to revive it:

  • File Form REG-21 within 30 days of cancellation order (extendable up to 90 days with valid reason, up to 180 in some cases)
  • Clear all pending returns + tax + fees first
  • Officer issues REG-22 (revocation) or REG-23 (rejection)
⚠️
Don't skip GSTR-10The final return is mandatory after cancellation. Skipping it = ₹10K penalty or 0.25% of turnover (whichever higher), even though your GSTIN is cancelled. File it.

What happens after cancellation

  • You can no longer issue tax invoices or collect GST
  • You can't claim ITC
  • You're still liable for any GST due for periods when you were registered
  • GSTIN is shown as "Cancelled" in the public lookup
  • Re-registration requires fresh REG-01 application (with a different GSTIN)

Cancelling cleanly is as important as registering cleanly. A botched cancellation hangs over you with penalties for years.
