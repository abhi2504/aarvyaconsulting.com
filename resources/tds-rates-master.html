TDS rates master — every section, every threshold

A bookmark-worthy reference for every TDS section, rate, and threshold. Updated for FY 25-26.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • TDS = Tax Deducted at Source. Deductor cuts a slice of payment, deposits to govt in deductee's PAN.
  • Deductor responsibilities: deduct correctly, deposit by 7th of next month, file quarterly return (24Q/26Q/27Q), issue certificate (Form 16/16A).
  • No PAN of deductee → higher TDS rate (typically 20%).
  • If deductee in lower tax bracket, they apply for lower-deduction certificate (Section 197).

Deductor compliance — the routine

  1. Obtain TAN

    Tax Deduction Account Number — apply via Form 49B at tin-nsdl.com.

  2. Deduct TDS at payment / credit

    Whichever earlier. Apply correct section + rate.

  3. Deposit by 7th of next month

    (30 April for March deductions). Use Challan ITNS 281. PAN of deductee + TAN of you required.

  4. File quarterly TDS return

    • 24Q — TDS on salary (Sec 192) — by 31 Jul / 31 Oct / 31 Jan / 31 May
    • 26Q — TDS on non-salary domestic payments — same dates
    • 27Q — TDS on payments to NRIs
    • 27EQ — TCS quarterly return

  5. Issue Form 16 / 16A

    Form 16: salary TDS, annual, by 15 Jun. Form 16A: non-salary, quarterly, within 15 days of return due date.

Penalties for non-compliance

  • Late deduction: 1% per month interest
  • Late deposit: 1.5% per month interest from date of deduction
  • Late filing of return: ₹200/day under Section 234E (capped at total TDS) + ₹10,000 to ₹1L penalty under 271H
  • Late issuance of Form 16/16A: ₹100/day
  • Failure to deduct: Section 40(a) — disallowance of expense in income tax + penalty

Special situations

Lower-deduction certificate (Section 197)

If TDS would exceed your final tax liability, apply for a Section 197 certificate. Deductor applies that lower rate. Useful for freelancers, exporters, NRIs.

Form 15G / 15H

Self-declaration to deductor (bank) that you won't have tax liability. Banks stop deducting TDS on interest. 15G — under 60; 15H — 60+.

For deductors: late TDS payment is more expensive than the TDS itself. Set up an internal "7th of every month" rule and never miss.
