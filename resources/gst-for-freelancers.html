GST for freelancers & consultants

Solo creator earning from India + overseas clients? GST has a specific shape for you. Here it is.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • If your annual receipts > ₹20 lakh (services threshold): GST mandatory.
  • Foreign clients ≠ trigger registration by themselves, but export of service rules apply once registered.
  • Marketplace freelancers (Upwork, Fiverr, etc.): only customer count is GST-relevant; turnover threshold still applies.
  • For India B2B clients: voluntary GST often helps — they prefer GST suppliers for ITC.

Three freelancer scenarios

Scenario 1 — All Indian clients, under ₹20L

Not mandatory. Voluntary is optional. Pros: B2B clients prefer GST suppliers. Cons: monthly filing chore + 18% on bills makes you costlier in price-sensitive segments.

Verdict: register voluntarily if 70%+ clients are GST-registered businesses. Skip otherwise.

Scenario 2 — All overseas clients (USD/EUR receipts)

Below ₹20L: not mandatory. Above ₹20L: mandatory (export of service is a "supply" too).

Voluntary registration even at ₹0 turnover often helps — input GST on co-working, internet, laptop, software → claim refund via RFD-01.

Use the LUT route to skip paying IGST upfront.

Scenario 3 — Mixed India + overseas

Treat each leg separately. Total receipts (₹ + foreign exchange in ₹ equivalent) count toward the ₹20L threshold.

How to invoice (registered freelancer)

  • Show your GSTIN, client's GSTIN (if applicable)
  • HSN/SAC code (for services, use SAC — 6-digit, e.g. 998313 for IT consulting)
  • Taxable value
  • CGST + SGST (intra-state) OR IGST (inter-state / overseas under LUT, show 0% with LUT ARN)
  • Place of supply (state code)
  • Invoice number (continuous series, FY-wise)

For overseas clients — export of service rules

To qualify as "export of service" (zero-rated):

  1. Supplier in India
  2. Recipient outside India
  3. Place of supply outside India
  4. Payment in convertible foreign exchange (USD/EUR/GBP credited via banking channels)
  5. Supplier and recipient not establishments of same person

All five must be satisfied. Get an FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) from your bank as proof.

💡
Payment platforms matterReceiving USD via Wise / Payoneer / direct wire? FIRC is straightforward. Receiving via PayPal which converts to INR before paying you? PayPal-generated certificate may not be accepted by all officers as FIRC — best to receive directly to your bank for full export-of-service compliance.

Most freelancers should register for GST somewhere between ₹15L and ₹20L turnover. Crossing the threshold mid-year is messier than registering proactively when you can see it coming.
