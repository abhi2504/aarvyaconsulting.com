Why is my ITR refund delayed?

Filed your return, expected refund in 2 weeks, it's been 2 months. Here are the 5 most common reasons — and how to fix each.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Check status: incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns → Track Status.
  • Top 5 causes: (1) ITR not e-verified, (2) bank account not pre-validated, (3) AIS / 26AS mismatch, (4) outstanding old demand, (5) Section 245 set-off.
  • Normal timeline: 1–4 weeks. Anything beyond 60 days = investigate.

The 5 main causes

Cause 1 — ITR not e-verified

Filing submits the return; e-verification activates it. Without verification within 30 days, ITR is invalid → no refund.

Fix: Verify via Aadhaar OTP / net banking / EVC / signed ITR-V to CPC.

Cause 2 — Bank account not pre-validated

Refund credits only to pre-validated bank accounts. If validation failed (name mismatch, IFSC error, account closed), refund holds.

Fix: Profile → My Bank Account → Add & validate fresh account. Re-issue refund request.

Cause 3 — AIS / 26AS mismatch flagged

If your AIS shows income you didn't report (FD interest, capital gains, etc.), system holds refund pending verification.

Fix: Compliance Portal → review flagged items → either amend ITR (revised / ITR-U) or submit explanation.

Cause 4 — Outstanding demand from prior years

Section 245 — system auto-adjusts current refund against any old confirmed demand.

Fix: Check pending demands at e-Filing → Pending Actions → Response to Outstanding Demand. Pay / challenge as appropriate.

Cause 5 — Scrutiny / processing hold

Manual review by officer (rare for salaried). Could be linked to high-value transactions, suspicious activity, or random selection.

Fix: No direct fix — respond promptly to any communication. Reach AO via Assessing Officer details on portal.

How to check refund status

  1. Login to e-Filing portal

  2. e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Filed Returns

  3. Click on the relevant AY's ITR

    Click "View Refund / Demand Status" or "Track Refund".

  4. Read status code

    • "Refund issued" — money sent (allow 7-10 working days)
    • "Refund credited" — in your bank already
    • "Refund failure" — failed to credit (bank issue, see Cause 2)
    • "Refund processed" — accepted, queued for issue
    • "Demand determined" — system says you owe instead
    • "Return processed; refund to be paid; not paid due to demand" — Section 245 at work

How to raise a grievance

  1. Login to e-Filing portal
  2. Grievances → Submit Grievance
  3. Pick category (Refund), provide ITR details, attach screenshots
  4. Usual resolution: 30 days

Escalation: e-Nivaran portal. Or call CPC: 1800-103-0025 / 1800-419-0025 (9 AM – 8 PM).

Interest on delayed refunds

If refund delayed beyond what's reasonable, you're entitled to interest under Section 244A:

  • 0.5% per month (6% annually) from 1 April of AY to date of grant
  • Auto-computed by system — you don't apply separately

Most "delayed" refunds aren't actually delayed — they're stuck on a tiny technicality. 10 minutes on the portal fixes 80% of cases.

FAQs

Could be (a) wrong bank details — re-issue with correct bank, (b) cheque-mode refund (NEFT failed) — wait or call CPC, (c) processing — wait 7-10 working days from "issued" status.

Average: 1-4 weeks after e-verification. Salaried + simple ITR-1 = often within 7 days. Complex ITR-2/3 = 2-6 weeks.

If you revised because of error: new processing starts. Could speed up OR delay. Don't revise unnecessarily.

Time limit: file ITR within 1 year of FY end to claim refund. Older years: usually no refund (limitation). Section 119(2)(b) applications for condonation are rare.

Update with new validated bank in your profile. Then on the refund status page, click "Refund Re-issue" → pick new bank.
