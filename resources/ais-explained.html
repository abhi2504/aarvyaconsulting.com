AIS & TIS — your complete financial fingerprint

The IT department knows more about your money than you think. The Annual Information Statement is the proof. Here's how to read it and respond.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • AIS (Annual Information Statement) — comprehensive record: salary, interest, dividends, MF, shares, property, foreign remittances, GST, etc.
  • TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary) — simplified version with values processed and grouped.
  • Access: incometax.gov.in → e-File → AIS.
  • Wrong entry? Submit feedback to remove / amend. The department processes these.

What's in your AIS

AIS aggregates data from many sources to give a single view of all your financial transactions:

  • Salary — from employer's TDS return
  • Interest — savings, FD, RD from banks
  • Dividends — from companies
  • Mutual fund — purchases, redemptions, dividend
  • Securities — broker transactions (shares, bonds, F&O)
  • Property — purchase / sale registrations
  • Foreign remittances — LRS, inward FEMA
  • GST turnover — from your GSTR-1 (for businesses)
  • Cash deposits / withdrawals — above ₹10L threshold
  • Credit card spends — high-value

AIS vs Form 26AS — what's the difference?

AspectForm 26ASAIS
CoverageTDS / TCS / Advance taxEverything financial
SourceDeductors' returns + tax paymentsDeductors + banks + brokers + MFs + registrars + SFT
Used forVerifying tax creditsVerifying income + cross-check ITR
FeedbackNoYes — you can dispute entries

How to download AIS

  1. Login to incometax.gov.in

  2. Click "Annual Information Statement"

    On your dashboard or via Services menu.

  3. Or navigate: Services → AIS

  4. Choose AY

    Pick the relevant Assessment Year.

  5. View / Download

    HTML view online or download PDF. Also CSV / JSON for export.

How to read AIS — the parts

  • Part A — General Information: PAN, name, address
  • Part B — TDS/TCS info: matches your 26AS
  • SFT transactions: Specified Financial Transactions reported by entities (banks, registrars, etc.)
  • Demand & refund history
  • Other information: salary, interest, dividend, capital gains, etc.

Giving feedback on incorrect entries

If AIS shows something wrong (e.g., interest you never earned, sale of property you don't own), submit feedback:

  1. Open the entry

    Click on the specific transaction in AIS.

  2. Click "Submit Feedback"

  3. Choose feedback option

    • "Information is correct"
    • "Information is partially correct"
    • "Information relates to other PAN/Year"
    • "Information is duplicate / not in nature of capital gain"
    • "Information is denied"

  4. Submit with reason

    Department processes feedback. Often within 1-2 weeks the AIS reflects the change.

💡
Use AIS BEFORE filing ITRDownload AIS before filing. Cross-check every entry against your actual records. Fix discrepancies via feedback. Then file ITR consistent with AIS. This prevents 90% of automated notices later.

Why AIS matters now

Since FY 21-22, ITR pre-fill comes from AIS. Discrepancies between filed ITR and AIS trigger:

  • Auto-notices under e-Verification scheme
  • Compliance portal flags
  • Refund holds
  • Scrutiny selection

The IT department now has more data than any tax authority before it. AIS is the visible tip. Treat it like a credit report — pull it, verify it, dispute errors.
