GST refund — when, how, how long

Your GST refund is your money. Here's how to claim it without leaving anything on the table.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Common refund situations: exports (zero-rated), inverted duty structure, excess balance in cash ledger, refund of input GST on capital goods (for exporters).
  • File Form RFD-01 on the GST portal.
  • Realistic timeline: 30–60 days (90% refund auto in 7 days for exporters; balance after verification).
  • Limitation period: 2 years from the relevant date.

When can you claim a GST refund?

Step-by-step refund process

  1. Confirm eligibility & period

    Identify the reason and the relevant period. Compute eligible refund amount based on prescribed formula (e.g. for exports: Net ITC × Export Turnover / Total Turnover).

  2. Login to gst.gov.in

    Services → Refunds → Application for Refund.

  3. Pick refund type

    Choose the right category from the dropdown — exports with IGST, exports under LUT, inverted duty, excess cash balance, etc.

  4. Fill RFD-01 with computation

    The form auto-pulls return data. Enter:

    • Period (tax period or financial year as applicable)
    • Amount of refund claimed (under each head — CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess)
    • Bank account for credit

  5. Attach supporting documents

    Vary by refund type. Typically:

    • Statement of invoices (Statement 3 for LUT exporters, etc.)
    • Shipping bills / FIRC for exports
    • Declaration that refund not passed on (Form RFD-09)
    • Self-certified bank account proof

  6. Submit with DSC / EVC

    You get an ARN (Application Reference Number).

  7. Provisional 90% refund for exporters

    If exports under LUT — 90% of claimed refund credited provisionally within 7 days. Balance after verification.

  8. Final verification

    Officer reviews. May issue deficiency memo (RFD-03) — respond within 15 days. If approved, refund order (RFD-06) issued and amount credited.

The deemed export refund

Sales to EOUs, Advance Authorisation holders, EPCG holders qualify as deemed exports. Either supplier or recipient can claim refund of GST paid. File via RFD-01 separately.

Common reasons refunds get delayed / rejected

⚠️
Top 5 refund killers1. Returns not filed for the claim period (file all GSTR-1 + 3B first). 2. ITC claimed not appearing in 2B → officer disallows. 3. Shipping bill / EGM mismatch with GSTR-1. 4. FIRC not received (proof of foreign exchange receipt). 5. Bank account details mismatch.

Limitation period

2 years from the relevant date. Relevant date varies:

  • Exports of goods — date of export (shipping bill date)
  • Exports of services — date of receipt of forex / date of invoice (whichever later)
  • Inverted duty — end of the FY in which claim arises
  • Excess paid — date of payment

Exporters who track their refund pipeline monthly never run out of working capital. Those who file once a year sit on lakhs blocked with the department.
