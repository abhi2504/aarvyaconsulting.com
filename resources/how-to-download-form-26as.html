How to download Form 26AS — step by step

Form 26AS is the government's master record of all the TDS, advance tax, and high-value transactions linked to your PAN. Here's how to get it in 2 minutes.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • Go to incometax.gov.in → login → e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Form 26AS
  • You'll be redirected to TRACES — click "View Tax Credit (Form 26AS)"
  • Pick the Assessment Year, format (HTML/PDF), download
  • Also check AIS (Annual Information Statement) — newer, more comprehensive version

What's in Form 26AS?

Form 26AS is your tax passbook. It lists:

  • Part A — TDS deducted on your salary, FD interest, professional fees, etc.
  • Part A1 — TDS where Form 15G/15H was filed (no actual deduction)
  • Part A2 — TDS on property sale (Section 194-IA)
  • Part B — TCS (Tax Collected at Source) — by sellers like jewellers, motor vehicle dealers
  • Part C — Advance tax / self-assessment tax you paid
  • Part D — Paid refunds
  • Part E — Specified Financial Transactions (SFT) — large purchases, deposits
  • Part F — TDS on rent, contractor payments, property purchases by you
  • Part G — Demand and refund details

Step-by-step download

  1. Log into the IT portal

    Go to incometax.gov.in. Click Login. Use PAN as username + your password.

  2. Navigate to Form 26AS

    e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Form 26AS. A consent screen appears — click Confirm. You'll be redirected to TRACES.

  3. On TRACES — click "View Tax Credit"

    You're now on tdscpc.gov.in. Click View Tax Credit (Form 26AS) from the top menu.

  4. Pick AY and format

    Select the Assessment Year (current AY = FY just ended). Format: HTML (online view) or PDF (download / print).

  5. Click View / Download

    Form 26AS opens / downloads. The PDF is password-protected — password is your PAN in lowercase + DOB in DDMMYYYY (e.g., abcde1234f15081990).

Bonus — also check AIS

From AY 2021-22 onwards, the IT department launched AIS (Annual Information Statement) — a more detailed version that includes:

  • Salary breakup
  • Dividend, interest, MF redemption
  • Share / MF / property transactions
  • Foreign remittances
  • GST turnover comparison

Access: incometax.gov.in → e-File → AIS.

💡
Reconcile before filingPull both 26AS and AIS before filing your ITR. If anything doesn't match your actual records (TDS, salary, interest), raise feedback in AIS or get the deductor to correct. Filing with mismatches triggers automated notices later.

What if 26AS shows wrong / missing TDS?

  1. Identify the deductor

    26AS shows the TAN of the deductor (employer/bank). Note it.

  2. Contact them

    Email/call asking them to file a TDS correction statement (revised quarterly TDS return). This is on them, not you.

  3. Wait for the next 26AS refresh

    Updates typically appear in 7–15 days after the correction filing.

  4. If unresolved

    Raise a grievance at the IT portal's e-Nivaran module. Or contact us — we'll handle the chase.

Don't file ITR before reconciling 26AS and AIS. 30 minutes here saves you weeks of notice-handling later.
