Advance tax — who pays, when, how

Pay-as-you-earn. Four instalments through the year. Miss them → 1% interest per month under Sections 234B/234C.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • You must pay advance tax if your total tax liability > ₹10,000 after TDS.
  • Four due dates: 15 Jun (15%), 15 Sep (45%), 15 Dec (75%), 15 Mar (100%).
  • Senior citizens (60+, no business income) and presumptive scheme filers (44AD/ADA) have relaxed rules.
  • Pay via challan ITNS 280 on tin-nsdl.com or e-Filing portal.

Who must pay

If your net tax payable (computed tax minus TDS already deducted) exceeds ₹10,000 in a financial year, you must pay advance tax.

Most affected:

  • Freelancers / consultants (high gross receipts, low TDS)
  • Business owners (no TDS source)
  • People with large capital gains (sold property/shares)
  • Salaried with significant other income (rent, FD interest)

The four due dates (general taxpayers)

Due dateCumulative tax to payThis-instalment %
15 June15% of annual tax15%
15 September45% of annual tax30%
15 December75% of annual tax30%
15 March100% of annual tax25%

Special rule — presumptive scheme (44AD / 44ADA / 44AE)

If you file under presumptive scheme, you need to pay full advance tax in one instalment by 15 March. No quarterly breakdown.

Penalty for shortfall — 234B and 234C

Section 234B — for total advance tax shortfall

If advance tax paid is < 90% of total tax liability, 1% interest per month on the shortfall from 1 April of AY until paid.

Section 234C — for instalment shortfalls

For each missed instalment cut-off, interest at 1% per month for 3 months on the shortfall.

Example: Should have paid ₹50K by 15 Jun but paid only ₹20K. Shortfall ₹30K × 1% × 3 months = ₹900 interest.

How to pay

  1. Estimate annual income

    By 31 May, project your full-year income. Use last year as baseline + known changes.

  2. Compute tax liability

    Apply slabs (old or new). Subtract eligible deductions. Get gross tax.

  3. Subtract TDS

    What's likely to be deducted at source from salary, FDs, professional fees, etc. Net tax = remaining advance tax to pay.

  4. Pay via Challan ITNS 280

    Go to tin-nsdl.com → Pay Taxes Online → Challan ITNS 280. Choose: (100) Advance Tax, AY, PAN, amount.

  5. Save the challan

    You'll get a receipt with BSR code + Challan serial number. Enter these in your ITR's tax-paid schedule.

Capital gains — special advance tax rule

For capital gains arising mid-year (e.g. you sold property in Aug), advance tax on that gain is due in the next instalment after the gain — not retroactively.

Sold in Aug: pay gain-tax by 15 Sep. Sold in Nov: pay by 15 Dec. Sold in Feb: pay by 15 Mar.

Advance tax isn't extra tax — it's just paying through the year instead of at filing. The 1% / month penalty for skipping it is way more painful than the cash-flow hit of paying on time.
