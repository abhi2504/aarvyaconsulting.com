ITR for NRIs — the complete guide

Indian property rent, FD interest, capital gains, dividends — NRIs need to file too. Here's how.

🪙 In 60 seconds
  • NRIs file ITR-2 (most common) or ITR-3 (if business income).
  • Only Indian-source income is taxable: rent, FD interest, capital gains on Indian assets, dividends, etc.
  • Use DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement) to avoid double tax.
  • Filing deadline: 31 July (same as residents). Foreign assets reportable in Schedule FA (if you're "resident" status).

Residential status — the foundation

You're a Non-Resident (NRI) for a financial year if you satisfy BOTH conditions in Section 6:

  • Less than 182 days in India during that FY, AND
  • Less than 60 days in India + 365 days in preceding 4 FYs

Indians going abroad for employment / on a ship qualify under relaxed rules (60 → 182 days).

The new category: RNOR & "deemed resident"

  • RNOR (Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident) — transition status for returning NRIs. Only Indian + foreign business income taxable; passive foreign income exempt for 2-3 years.
  • Deemed resident (from FY 20-21) — Indian citizen with India-source income > ₹15L and not liable to tax in any other country. Taxed as RNOR.

DTAA — avoiding double tax

India has DTAAs with 90+ countries (US, UK, UAE, Singapore, etc.). DTAA helps in two ways:

  1. Lower TDS rate — claim DTAA rate (often 10-15%) instead of domestic 30%+. Submit Form 10F + TRC + self-declaration to your deductor.
  2. Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) — if same income is taxed both in India and your country of residence, claim credit via Form 67 (filed before ITR).

Form 10F — Tax Residency Self-Declaration

Required to claim DTAA benefits. Submit to Indian payer (tenant, broker, bank) so they apply DTAA TDS rates.

  • Online via incometax.gov.in (mandatory since 2022)
  • Need a TRC (Tax Residency Certificate) from your country of residence
  • Valid for one FY

Indian property — selling as NRI

  • Buyer must deduct TDS at 20% (long-term) or 30% (short-term) under Section 195
  • Plus surcharge + cess (effective ~22-24% LTCG)
  • Section 54, 54F, 54EC exemptions available
  • Get lower-deduction certificate (Section 197) from IT department to reduce TDS to actual liability
  • Repatriation: up to USD 1 million / FY allowed from sale proceeds; needs CA certificate Form 15CA + 15CB

Filing flow for NRIs

  1. Establish residential status

    Compute days in India for the FY. ITR has a schedule to declare.

  2. List all Indian-source income

    Rent, interest, dividends, capital gains, business income (if any), etc.

  3. Match with Form 26AS / AIS

    All TDS deducted by tenants, banks, brokers should reflect there.

  4. Compute tax (no slab benefit for most income — flat rates apply on capital gains, dividends)

  5. Claim DTAA relief via Form 67 (if applicable)

  6. File ITR-2 (or ITR-3 if business)

  7. E-verify via Aadhaar OTP / DSC / sending signed ITR-V to CPC Bengaluru

NRIs often overpay tax because tenants/banks/brokers apply maximum TDS. File ITR diligently — refunds are routine and substantial.
